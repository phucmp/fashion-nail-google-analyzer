Executive Summary

Fashion Nails & Spa VIP's story is one of remarkable transformation. Under previous ownership, the salon averaged just 3.0 stars with consistent complaints about rudeness and hygiene. Since MT took over in October 2024, the rating has risen to 4.9 — one of the most dramatic turnarounds in the valley.

MT herself is the single biggest driver of loyalty. Mentioned in 21% of all recent reviews with near-perfect ratings, she has personally turned first-time visitors into lifelong regulars. Tracy (83 mentions), Jay (46), and Anna (35) are her strongest supporting cast.

The primary risk is appointment reliability. A growing cluster of recent 1-star reviews comes from clients who booked MT specifically, arrived on time, and were handed to another tech. Managing client expectations around her schedule is the most critical operational challenge ahead.
